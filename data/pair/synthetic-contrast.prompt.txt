Write 20 different messages that customers of a banking app might send to customer support.
These two intents are easy to confuse:
- verify_my_identity: The customer asks how to verify their identity or what documents and steps are needed — they accept that it must be done and want to know how, not why.
- why_verify_identity: The customer asks why they have to verify their identity at all, or objects to being asked — they want the reason, not the steps.
Every message has the intent verify_my_identity. Each message must belong to verify_my_identity and could not be read as why_verify_identity.
A message may mention that verification was asked for, but it asks only how to do it, what to submit, or where the customer is in the process.
Vary the length from 4 to 30 words.
Style for this batch: short and blunt, a few words to one sentence.
Vary the wording and situation. One message per array item, no numbering.
