Write 20 different messages that customers of a banking app might send to customer support.
These two intents are easy to confuse:
- card_arrival: The customer has already waited for a card they ordered, it has not come, and they ask where it is — a complaint about a card that is late or missing, not a question about normal delivery times.
- card_delivery_estimate: The customer asks how long card delivery takes or when a card will arrive, as a question about timing or delivery options — they are not complaining that a card is late or missing.
Every message has the intent card_arrival. Each message must belong to card_arrival and could not be read as card_delivery_estimate.
At least half of the messages must express the intent without the words that most obviously signal it, in particular the words that make up the name card_arrival.
Vary the length from 4 to 30 words.
Style for this batch: short and blunt, a few words to one sentence.
Vary the wording and situation. One message per array item, no numbering.
